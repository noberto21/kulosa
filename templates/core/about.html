About KULOSA

KULOSA helps people in Kenya find things to do, from concerts and matches to campus nights and community days, and helps organizers get the word out.

Anyone can browse. Registered users will be able to submit events, and each submission is reviewed before it is published. Tickets are sold by organizers and their chosen ticketing partners; KULOSA links to them and does not process payments.
