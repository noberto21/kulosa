Privacy Policy

KULOSA is committed to protecting your personal data and upholding your fundamental right to privacy in accordance with the laws of Kenya.

Effective Date: October 15, 2026 • Governing Law: Republic of Kenya (Act No. 24 of 2019) • Oversight: ODPC Kenya

Privacy Summary: How We Protect Your Data

This policy explains how KULOSA processes personal data in strict compliance with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 (DPA) and guidelines from the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC):

  • Data Minimization: We only collect what is strictly necessary to enable your account, manage event listings, and provide discovery services.
  • Privacy-by-Design on Media: When posters or logos are uploaded, our systems automatically re-encode images and permanently strip all embedded EXIF metadata (including camera details and GPS coordinates).
  • No Data Brokering: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information or attendee habits to third-party advertisers or data brokers.
  • Your Statutory Rights: You possess enforceable rights under Kenyan law to access, rectify, port, object to, and request deletion of your personal data at any time.

1. Statutory Framework & Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to all personal data collected, stored, processed, or transferred by KULOSA through our website (kulosa.co.ke), mobile services, and related applications.

Our operations, data handling architectures, and privacy safeguards are structured in full compliance with the Data Protection Act, 2019 (Act No. 24 of 2019, Laws of Kenya), the Data Protection (General) Regulations, 2021, the Data Protection (Registration of Data Controllers and Data Processors) Regulations, 2021, and the Data Protection (Complaints Handling Procedure and Enforcement) Regulations, 2021.

For the purposes of the Data Protection Act, 2019, the Data Controller responsible for determining the purposes and means of processing your personal data is:

Entity: KULOSA (Event Discovery Platform)

Data Protection Inquiries: privacy@kulosa.co.ke
General Support: support@kulosa.co.ke

3. Data Protection Principles We Uphold (Section 25 DPA)

In accordance with Section 25 of the Data Protection Act, 2019, KULOSA adheres to the core statutory principles of data protection:

Lawfulness, Fairness & Transparency

Personal data is processed lawfully, fairly, and transparently, respecting your constitutional right to privacy under Article 31(c) and (d) of the Constitution of Kenya.

Purpose Limitation

Data is collected only for explicit, specified, and legitimate event discovery purposes and never further processed in an incompatible manner.

Data Minimization

We restrict data collection to what is adequate, relevant, and strictly necessary for our discovery and dashboard functions.

Accuracy & Data Integrity

Every reasonable step is taken to ensure that inaccurate personal data is erased or rectified promptly through your profile settings.

Storage Limitation

Personal data is kept in an identifiable form for no longer than necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected.

Integrity & Confidentiality

Technical and organizational security controls protect data against unauthorized access, loss, destruction, or compromise.

4. Categories of Personal Data We Collect

We collect personal data directly from you when you interact with our platform:

A. Account Information

When you register an account to list events or save favorites, we collect your full name, email address (normalized for case-insensitive security), optional phone number, and a cryptographically hashed password (we never store plain-text passwords).

B. Organizer Profile & Listing Information

If you publish events, we collect your organizer brand name, public contact email, public contact phone, website link, event venue names, physical addresses, counties, towns, and external ticketing links.

C. User Activity & Interaction

Events you bookmark/save to your personal list and moderation reports submitted regarding inaccurate or deceptive listings.

D. Technical & Security Logs

Temporary network logs, including IP addresses (used for automated rate limiting to prevent credential-stuffing attacks and brute-force logins), browser user-agent strings, and essential security tokens.

Special Category Data Notice: KULOSA does not intentionally collect, store, or process sensitive personal data as defined under Section 43 of the DPA (such as biometric data, health data, genetic data, or religious/philosophical beliefs) unless voluntarily made public by an organizer in an event title or description.

5. Lawful Bases for Processing (Section 30 DPA)

Under Section 30 of the Kenya Data Protection Act, we must establish a statutory lawful basis before processing personal data. We rely on the following bases:

  • Performance of a Contract: Processing necessary to deliver the services you request, including creating your account, publishing your event listings, and managing your dashboard.
  • Consent: Explicit consent given when registering your account, opting into newsletters, or voluntarily providing optional contact numbers. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal Obligation: Processing required to comply with Kenyan statutory mandates, court orders, or lawful investigations by law enforcement bodies under the Computer Misuse and Cybercrimes Act, 2018.
  • Legitimate Interests: Protecting platform integrity, detecting and mitigating fraudulent listings, preventing automated spam, rate-limiting malicious traffic, and ensuring server availability.

6. Purposes for Which We Process Your Data

We process your personal data solely for explicit, lawful purposes:

  • Enabling account creation, authentication, and secure password resets;
  • Publishing, reviewing, and categorizing event listings submitted by organizers;
  • Allowing attendees to bookmark events and view saved listings in their personal portal;
  • Sending critical administrative notifications (such as event submission approvals, rejection reasons, or security alerts);
  • Enforcing community safety by reviewing flagged event reports and mitigating spam;
  • Maintaining platform availability and defending against denial-of-service and brute-force attacks.

7. Poster Uploads & Privacy-by-Design (EXIF / GPS Stripping)

In line with Section 41 of the Data Protection Act (Data Protection by Design and by Default), KULOSA implements built-in technical safeguards for media uploads:

Automated EXIF & GPS Removal:

Photographs taken with mobile phones and modern cameras frequently contain hidden Exchangeable Image File (EXIF) metadata, including precise geographical GPS coordinates, timestamps, and device serial numbers. KULOSA automatically re-encodes all uploaded posters and logos into fresh images, permanently scrubbing all EXIF and location data before saving them to disk.

Additionally, uploaded filenames are discarded and replaced with randomized unique identifiers (UUIDs) to prevent unintentional exposure of personal information contained in original file names.

8. Data Sharing & Third-Party Processors

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade personal data. We only share personal data in the following strictly defined circumstances:

  • Public Event Listings: Information you explicitly include in an event listing or public organizer profile (such as organizer name, contact email, phone, and ticketing links) is published publicly to enable attendees to discover your event.
  • Ticketing Providers: When users click external booking links, they navigate directly to third-party ticketing vendors (e.g., organizer ticketing portals or payment processors). KULOSA does not transmit attendee user account credentials or personal profile data to these external vendors.
  • Essential Infrastructure Providers: Trusted third-party processors providing hosting, email delivery (transactional notices), or technical infrastructure under strict contractual confidentiality and data protection agreements.
  • Legal Mandates & Law Enforcement: We may disclose information if required to do so by a valid court order, warrant, or statutory requirement under Kenyan law, or in response to formal inquiries from the ODPC.

9. Cross-Border Transfers of Personal Data (Section 48 DPA)

Under Section 48 and Section 49 of the Kenya Data Protection Act, personal data may only be transferred outside Kenya where adequate safeguards are established.

While KULOSA's primary audience and operations are situated in Kenya, server hosting, cloud database storage, or automated transactional email services may utilize infrastructure located outside the Republic of Kenya. When such transfers occur, KULOSA ensures that:

  • The recipient jurisdiction has equivalent or adequate data protection laws; or
  • Appropriate contractual clauses and data transfer agreements are in place; or
  • The transfer is necessary for the performance of our contract with you.

10. Security Measures & Storage Limitation

Technical Safeguards: In compliance with Section 41 of the DPA, we employ multi-layered technical measures including encrypted HTTPS/TLS in transit, PBKDF2/Argon2 password hashing, Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) protection, automated rate-limiting on sensitive endpoints (login, registration, password resets), and restricted staff permissions.

Retention Policy: We retain personal data only for as long as your account remains active or as needed to maintain historical event archives. If you close your account or request deletion, your account credentials and personal contact details are deleted or irreversibly anonymized, subject to statutory record-retention requirements under Kenyan law.

11. Your Statutory Data Subject Rights (Section 26 DPA)

As a data subject under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you have specific, legally enforceable rights:

1. Right to Be Informed

You have the right to be informed of the use to which your personal data is put (as detailed in this Privacy Policy).

2. Right of Access

You have the right to access and receive confirmation of your personal data in our custody without undue delay.

3. Right to Rectification / Correction

You have the right to request the correction of false, inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated personal data.

4. Right to Deletion / Erasure

You have the right to request the deletion or erasure of personal data that is false, misleading, or no longer necessary for processing.

5. Right to Object

You have the right to object to the processing of all or part of your personal data, including for direct marketing purposes.

6. Right to Data Portability

You have the right to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our Data Protection desk at privacy@kulosa.co.ke. We will respond to your request within the statutory timeline prescribed by the DPA (ordinarily within 21 days).

12. Cookies & Technical Logging

KULOSA uses minimal, functional cookies necessary to deliver secure platform features:

  • Session Cookies (sessionid): Keeps you logged in as you navigate between pages.
  • Security Tokens (csrftoken): Protects your forms and submissions against Cross-Site Request Forgery attacks.

We do not deploy intrusive third-party behavioral tracking cookies or sell your browsing history to advertising networks.

13. Children’s Privacy (Section 33 DPA)

In accordance with Section 33 of the Data Protection Act, 2019, KULOSA incorporates safeguards regarding the processing of personal data relating to children.

Our platform does not knowingly collect personal data from individuals under 18 years of age without verifiable consent from a parent or legal guardian. If we learn that personal data of a minor has been collected without parental consent, we will take immediate steps to delete that information from our active databases.
