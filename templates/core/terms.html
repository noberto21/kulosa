4. Organizer Submissions & Listing Standards

When you submit an event for publication on KULOSA, you act as an "Organizer" and agree to adhere strictly to the following standards:

Truth in Details

Provide authentic event titles, realistic descriptions, correct Kenyan counties and towns, and accurate venue addresses. Misrepresenting venues, artists, or amenities is prohibited.

Accurate Pricing & Times

Paid events must state true minimum prices in Kenyan Shillings (KES). All event dates and start/end times must be in East Africa Time (EAT) and reflect real schedules.

Valid Booking Links

External ticketing URLs must point directly to legitimate ticketing platforms (e.g. M-PESA paybill pages, authorized ticketing vendors, or official registration forms) with secure HTTPS/HTTP links.

Licensing & Regulatory Compliance

Organizers are solely responsible for securing all requisite statutory permits, including county government event permits, NEMA noise licenses, and music copyright clearances (e.g. MCSK/PRISK/KAMP).

Review & Workflow: All submitted events undergo administrative review before being published. If you modify a published event, it returns to review status to ensure continued quality and accuracy. KULOSA reserves the unconditional right to approve, reject, or request revisions to any submission.

Cancellations & Postponements: If your event is postponed, relocated, or cancelled, you must immediately update or cancel the listing via your KULOSA dashboard and inform ticket holders through your primary communication channels.

5. Poster & Media Upload Guidelines

To maintain high visual standards and protect community members, all uploaded posters and logos must comply with our technical and safety parameters:

  • Accepted Formats: Genuine JPEG, PNG, or WebP image files only, up to a maximum file size of 5 MB and maximum dimensions of 6,000 pixels.
  • Prohibited Files: SVG, GIF, executable scripts, and disguised file types are strictly rejected by our automated upload pipeline.
  • Privacy Sanitization: For your privacy, KULOSA automatically strips EXIF metadata (including embedded GPS coordinates and camera models) and re-encodes images upon storage.
  • Content Restrictions: Posters must not contain explicit adult content, graphic violence, defamatory slurs, hate speech, or counterfeit commercial trademarks.
